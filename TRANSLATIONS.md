## 1. At the market

*A shopper buys fruit and bread from a stall.*  
**A** = Shopper · **B** = Fruit seller · [▶ video](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/kingmk3r/palanu@main/dialogues/dialogue-1-market.mp4) · [audio](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/kingmk3r/palanu@main/dialogues/dialogue-1-market.mp3) · [subtitles](dialogues/dialogue-1-market.srt)

| # | | Palanu | English |
|---:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Shopper** | Salama, sawi! Ca tu ka tene kiwawo sinu? | *Hello, friend! Do you have fresh fruit?* |
| 2 | **Fruit seller** | Ka! Cu kiwawo pu ta ka sinu, sa to ka pemu. Cu ku sile! | *Yes! My fruit is fresh and sweet. Take a look!* |
| 3 | **Shopper** | Peya pu kiwawo soluni ka sama co? | *What is the price of the yellow fruit?* |

| 7 | **Shopper** | Tane! Cu tu ku tene solu poni! | *Thanks! Have a good day!* |

## 4. A tense moment at work

*A missing report, an apology, and a fix.*

| # | | Palanu | English |
|---:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Coworker** | Cun Ana cun pun, pemiwo pu tu ke ce kome pa ta. Cu puye ka sama co? | *Ana, your report never reached me. What's the problem?* |
| 2 | **Ana** | Ta ka seni ponino pen ti. Cu penawe pu ta ke tana. | *I feel terrible about this. My computer died.* |
| 3 | **Coworker** | Kume ke kiyu. Cu te ke caye pemiwo pi solu ti. | *The boss was angry. She needed the report today.* |

| 6 | **Ana** | Ka. Cu ta ko kalo to pi solulu ti. Cu ta ka tane tu pan so tu ke tewo. | *Yes. I'll have it done this evening. Thank you for waiting.* |
| 7 | **Coworker** | Sala, sawi. Cu tan ka kuyu. | *It's all right, friend. We're a team.* |

## 5. A cosmic talk show: stars, black holes and the expanding universe

*A talk-show host who is an astrophysicist, and a curious, funny co-host, talk about stars, black holes and the expanding universe.*  
**A** = Host · **B** = Co-host · [▶ video](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/kingmk3r/palanu@main/dialogues/dialogue-5-astrophysics.mp4) · [audio](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/kingmk3r/palanu@main/dialogues/dialogue-5-astrophysics.mp3) · [subtitles](dialogues/dialogue-5-astrophysics.srt)

| # | | Palanu | English |
|---:|:--|:--|:--|
| 1 | **Host** | Salama, somon pu tela! Cu pi solu ti pun tan ko palayu pen seyon sa sewanu. | *Greetings, people of Earth! Today we're talking about the stars and the universe.* |
| 2 | **Co-host** | Ta ka kono kowa kan pen seyon. Cu seyo ka sama co? | *I know absolutely nothing about stars. So what is a star?* |
| 3 | **Host** | Seyo ka sama pilo tawama pi sela suma. Cu pi kole pu to pun seyo ka tali cun atom cun tawano pa cun atom cun tawamo. | *A star is a gigantic fire in the night sky. Deep in its heart, the star fuses small atoms into bigger atoms.* |
| 4 | **Co-host** | Cu cun atom cun tawamo ke pelo pa tolo co? | *So where did those bigger atoms go?* |
| 5 | **Host** | Pi so seyo tawa ke tana pun to ke pelema, sa cun atom cun ke pelo pa sewanu. Cu pelu pu suyu piloni pu tu ke kome pe seyo! Cu tan sewa ka sama seyonan. Cu ku pena pen to! | *When a big star died, it exploded, and its atoms flew out into the universe. The iron in your blood came from a star! We are all made of star pieces. Think about that!* |
| 6 | **Co-host** | Cowi! Cu ta ka sama seyo! Cu ta ke kono to pume. Cu pune sumani ka sama co? | *Whoa! So I'm a star! I knew it all along. And what's a black hole?* |
| 7 | **Host** | Pi so seyo tawama ka tana pun to ki sama pune sumani. Cu pole puma pu to ka tawama, sa kolo ka ce pole pelo pe to. | *When a giant star dies, it can become a black hole. Its gravity is so enormous that not even light can escape it.* |
| 8 | **Co-host** | Ca pune sumani ki tuma ta? Cu ta ka kowe! | *Could a black hole grab me? I'm scared!* |
| 9 | **Host** | Ka ce, sawi. Cu to ka tayoma pe tan. Cu pole puma ka kuluya tolo, sa kolo ka tiye tolo kuluyo. | *No, my friend. It's very far from us. Gravity bends space itself, and light follows that bent space.* |
| 10 | **Co-host** | Ta ke pena so kolo ka pelo pi tali sola! | *I thought light only travels in a straight line!* |
| 11 | **Host** | Cu sewanu ka tawale! Cu pi so tu ka sile seyo tayo pun tu ka sile kolo so co ke pelo kulu kolo ken kan kan kan. Cu tu ka sile temo pume! | *And the universe is expanding! When you look at a distant star, you see light that traveled a thousand light-years. You are looking into the past!* |
| 12 | **Co-host** | Sewanu ka tawale, sa tanu pu ta ka tawale tuye, pan kula pulu! Tane, sawi! | *The universe is expanding, and so is my waistline, thanks to all the food! Thanks, friend!* |

## Interlinear glosses

Line 1 is the sentence, line 2 splits it into morphemes, and line 3 is the gloss, as in the [specification](spec.md). Name frames are shown as `cun…cun`.

**Greeting and a yes/no question** (dialogue 1, line 1)

```
Salama, sawi! Ca tu ka tene kiwawo sinu?

salama     sawi    ca  tu   ka   tene  kiwawo    sinu
sala-ma    sawi    ca  tu   ka   tene  kiwa-wo   sinu
peace-AUG  friend  Q   2SG  PRS  have  tree-RES  new
```
> Hello, friend! Do you have fresh fruit?

**Negation, imperatives and coordination** (dialogue 2, line 5)

```
Puye ka ce tawa. Cu ku pinu suyu pulu, sa ku temu pulu.

puye     ka   ce   tawa  cu   ku   pinu   suyu   pulu  sa   ku   temu   pulu
puye     ka   ce   tawa  cu   ku   pinu   suyu   pulu  sa   ku   temu   pulu
problem  PRS  NEG  big   SEP  IMP  drink  water  many  and  IMP  sleep  many
```
> It's not a big problem. Drink lots of water, and sleep a lot.

**Contrast with *su* "but", and a coined word** (dialogue 3, line 5)

```
Culewe ka kuwi, su peya pu to ka tawa. Cu tan ku pelo po pelowe!

culewe   ka   kuwi  su   peya   pu  to    ka   tawa  cu   tan  ku   pelo
cule-we  ka   kuwi  su   peya   pu  to    ka   tawa  cu   tan  ku   pelo
fly-INS  PRS  fast  but  money  of  that  PRS  big   SEP  1PL  IMP  go

po    pelowe
po    pelo-we
with  go-INS
```
> The plane is fast, but it's expensive. Let's take the car!

**A context phrase closed by *pun*, and a name frame** (dialogue 4, line 1)

```
Cun Ana cun pun, pemiwo pu tu ke ce kome pa ta. Cu puye ka sama co?

cun ana cun  pun  pemiwo     pu  tu   ke   ce   kome  pa  ta   cu   puye
cun ana cun  pun  pemi-wo    pu  tu   ke   ce   kome  pa  ta   cu   puye
NAME         CTX  write-RES  of  2SG  PST  NEG  come  to  1SG  SEP  problem

ka   sama  co
ka   sama  co
PRS  be    what
```
> Ana, your report never reached me. What's the problem?

**Subordinate clause, compound nouns, and negation** (dialogue 5, line 7)

```
Pi so seyo tawama ka tana pun to ki sama pune sumani. Cu pole puma pu to ka tawama, sa kolo ka ce pole pelo pe to.

pi  so    seyo  tawama   ka   tana  pun  to    ki   sama  pune  sumani
pi  so    seyo  tawa-ma  ka   tana  pun  to    ki   sama  pune  suma-ni
at  that  star  big-AUG  PRS  die   CTX  that  HYP  be    door  night-SIM

cu   pole  puma   pu  to    ka   tawama   sa   kolo   ka   ce   pole  pelo
cu   pole  puma   pu  to    ka   tawa-ma  sa   kolo   ka   ce   pole  pelo
SEP  can   heavy  of  that  PRS  big-AUG  and  light  PRS  NEG  can   go

pe    to
pe    to
from  that
```
> When a giant star dies, it can become a black hole. Its gravity is so enormous that not even light can escape it.

Abbreviations: 1SG/2SG/3SG = I/you/he-she, 1PL = we, PRS/PST/FUT = present/past/future, IMP = imperative, HYP = hypothetical, Q = yes/no question, SEP = statement opener, NEG = not, CTX = context closer, AUG = augmentative, OPP = opposite, INS = means/tool, CAUS = causative, AGT = agent, RES = result, SIM = "like", CESS = "stop", PL = plural, NAME = name frame.
